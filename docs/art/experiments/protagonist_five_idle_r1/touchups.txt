# Local Aseprite correction manifest

Every correction is on the independent `Local pixel corrections` layer.
The raw PixelLab layer remains untouched in every `.aseprite` master.

## Lower-leg skin clusters

Only the exposed lower-leg and foot boxes, from y=88 through y=127, are sampled.
The source's substantial dark off-palette clusters are replaced with the two exact
B skin-dark swatches according to source brightness.

| Direction | Zones | Dark-source pixels | B replacement |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 0° | x52–59 and x69–76 | 105 | #99807B / #C5A891 |
| 45° | x54–61 and x67–74 | 133 | #99807B / #C5A891 |
| 90° | x59–68 | 76 | #99807B / #C5A891 |
| 135° | x55–64 and x67–76 | 265 | #99807B / #C5A891 |
| 180° | x53–61 and x67–75 | 244 | #99807B / #C5A891 |

The 135° green-grey calf clusters and the 180° copper calf clusters are included
in these boxes. Bright skin is preserved. Garment pixels are outside all zones.

## Back crown

At 180° only, 22 opaque pixels in the upper-left crown at x55–61, y5–11 are
lifted to B hair mid-dark #454E48. The upper-right crown remains unchanged.
